Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

This policy explains what the ELEV8 Trading Journal ("ELEV8", "we", "us") collects when you use elev8tradingjournal.com and the ELEV8 app, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have. ELEV8 is run by Kacper Jasinski in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, date of birth (we work out your age from it), email address and password. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider in scrambled (hashed) form; we can never see them.
  • Your journal: everything you add, including trades (pairs, direction, prices, lot size, risk, result, fees, dates and times), setups, sessions, notes, mistakes, emotions, trading plan rules, chart screenshots, trading accounts (type, broker or prop firm, size, platform, nickname), payouts and payout certificates, and your settings.
  • Email preferences: whether you've turned on the weekly review email.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

We don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don't use cookies for tracking. The app saves a copy of your journal and your sign-in session in your browser's storage on your device so it works quickly and offline.

We never ask for your broker or prop firm login, and we can't place trades on your behalf.

Why we use it

  • To run the journal for you (performing our agreement with you): creating your account, syncing your journal between devices, showing your stats, insights and weekly review, and storing your screenshots and certificates.
  • To send account emails you need, such as confirming your email, resetting your password or confirming an email change.
  • To send your weekly review email, only if you turn it on (your consent). You can turn it off in Settings at any time.
  • To keep ELEV8 safe and working (our legitimate interests): preventing abuse, fixing problems and handling bug reports you send.
  • To check your age: ELEV8 is for people aged 13 and over.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone for marketing.

Who helps us run ELEV8

These providers process data for us, only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage for your account and journal.
  • Resend: sends our emails (account emails and the weekly review).
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages and GitHub Actions): hosts the website and runs the weekly review job.
  • Cloudflare: domain name and network services for elev8tradingjournal.com.
  • Google Fonts: the fonts on our pages load from Google, which receives your IP address when they do.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States or other countries outside the UK. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and journal for as long as you have an account. You can delete your trades yourself at any time in Settings. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account, journal, screenshots and certificates within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something. Server logs held by our providers are kept for a short period under their own policies.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy of it in a portable format. You can export your whole journal yourself from Settings (Excel, CSV or JSON) and correct your name, date of birth and email in your profile. Where we rely on your consent, such as the weekly review email, you can withdraw it at any time.

To use any of these rights, or to delete your account, email privacy@elev8tradingjournal.com from the address on your account. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Security

Your journal is private to your account: our database only lets each signed-in person read and change their own trades, settings and files. Connections to ELEV8 are encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else, and sign out on shared devices.

Children

ELEV8 isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
