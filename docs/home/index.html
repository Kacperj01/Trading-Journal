    Questions, answered.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?+

    Yes. Open elev8tradingjournal.com in Safari or Chrome and tap Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like a normal app, and your trades sync to your desktop.

    Which prop firms are supported?+

    FTMO, Funding Pips and Alpha Capital come built in, with every program's profit target, daily loss, max loss, trading days, news and weekend rules. Trading with another firm? Add it as a prop account by name and ELEV8 tracks the balance and P&L. More firms are being added.

    Does it connect to my broker?+

    No. ELEV8 never asks for your broker login. You log trades yourself or import an MT4/MT5 statement, so nothing can ever place a trade on your account.

    Can I track backtests?+

    Yes. Add a Backtest account and log your replay trades separately, so they never mix with your live or prop results.

    Are fees and commission included?+

    Yes. Add the fees on a trade and they're taken off the result, so your P&L and win rate match what actually hit your account.

    When does my week reset?+

    Every Monday. Your Overview and stats start a fresh week, and every past week stays saved. Use the filters to look at last week, a custom range or all time.

    What counts as a breakeven?+

    Any result within a tenth of your risk (0.1R) either side of zero. Breakevens are kept out of your win rate so a scratched trade doesn't count as a loss.

    Can I bring in my old trades?+

    Yes. Import an MT4 or MT5 account statement (.htm or .xlsx), or a CSV, Excel or JSON file. You can export everything the same way.

    Does it work without signal?+

    You can keep logging with a bad connection. Your trades are saved on the device and sync as soon as you're back online.

    Can I change the currency?+

    Yes. Pick your currency symbol in Settings and every figure in the journal uses it.

    Is there a light mode?+

    Yes. Dark is the default, and you can switch to light mode from the sidebar or Settings.

    How does the weekly email work?+

    Switch it on in Settings and every Sunday evening you get your week's review by email, from reviews@elev8tradingjournal.com: your results, what worked, what cost you and what to focus on next.

    Who can see my journal?+

    Only you. Your data is private to your account, and you can export a full backup from Settings at any time.

    Do you give trading signals?+

    No. ELEV8 is a journal and an education brand. Everything here is for education only and is not financial advice.
